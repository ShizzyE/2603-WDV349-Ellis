## Project Structure
Structuring a project is more than just having the right folders in place. This can go into how a website is broken down into components and even data that is for placeholders.

Key Findings 🔎 :
1. There are multiple different ways you can structure your web project. (Hierarchy, Organize by Feature, or even Framework specific)
2. Breaking your app into reusable components makes your code cleaner, easier to maintain, and faster to build with
3. Planning your structure ahead of time helps prevent confusion and makes scaling your app much easier later on.

<br>

## Data Modeling
Data modeling focuses on how information is structured, stored, and managed within an application to ensure it is organized and easy to work with. 

Key Findings 🔎 :
1. Data modeling is the process of defining how data is structured and organized within an application (such as users, tasks, or notes for my project)
2. In FastAPI, Pydantic models are used to validate and structure data being sent to and from the API
3. Good data models help ensure consistency, reduce errors, and make it easier to connect your backend to a database

<br>

## Deploying a website/ app
When you deploy a website or application, it becomes accessible to users or testers through the internet rather than just running locally. 

Key Findings 🔎 :
1. The frontend and backend of a web app can be deployed separately for more flexibility.
2. Tools like Docker, CI/CD pipelines, and platforms such as Vercel, Render, or AWS can simplify and automate deployment
3. Preparing for deployment includes handling environment variables, securing APIs, and ensuring your app runs efficiently in production
