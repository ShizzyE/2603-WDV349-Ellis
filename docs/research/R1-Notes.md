## AI Agents
An AI Agent is a tool that helps the user better communicate with AI. It's like having a professional research assistant that maximizes a regular AI prompt capabilities.

*  AI agents do more than simple AI model outputs. They are able to provide complex, detailed, and actionable responses by analyzing, refining, and taking actions on the information they gather. 
* AI Agents can be specialized for different tasks or industries. Here are some examples :
 - Workplaces (e.g., project management)
 - Research (e.g., gathering and summarizing data)
 - Data analysis (e.g., statistical analysis & reporting)
* AI Agents have the abilities to observe, reason, self refine, plan, and more which makes them extremely valuable in work flows and work environments.

<br>

## AI APIs 
Like any other API an AI API can connect to your app/ software and be called to use. AI APIs allow you to use powerful AI tools and or softwares inside of your app.

* AI APIs eliminate the need to understand complex AI algorithms.
* There are many types of AI APIs on the internet, but some of the most known are Speech Recognition, Generative, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) APIs.
* AI APIs can be cost effective due to how scalable they are.

<br>

## AI Assisted Coding
AI Assisted coding is you walking step by step with AI while completing an app. This process is easy to confuse with vibe coding where you prompt the AI and it gives you a full app and you do not coding at all.

* AI Assisted coding tremendously speeds up workflows when used correctly.
* As a developer it is best to utilize AI when coding, but also understand the fundamentals of the language you're coding in to do so effectively. 
* Having things planned out before hand can help you code easier with AI so you know the end goal you want.
